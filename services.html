TV SERVICE, THOUGHTFULLY DONE

What can we
help you with?

We work on LED and LCD televisions and a broad range of common faults. Every repair starts with understanding the issue and assessing the TV; the appropriate solution depends on the model and the fault.

01 / REPAIR

LED TV Repair

Assessment and repair for LED television faults, including picture, lighting and power issues.

02 / REPAIR

LCD TV Repair

Experienced troubleshooting for LCD televisions with display, audio, connectivity or startup problems.

03 / SETUP

TV Installation

Help with setting up your television, connecting devices and getting the basics configured.

04 / SETUP

TV Uninstallation

Careful disconnection and uninstallation when it is time to move or reposition your television.

05 / CARE

TV Maintenance

General checks and maintenance guidance to help keep your television in good working order.

06 / DISPLAY

Flickering, blur & display problems

Diagnosis and repair for screen flickering, blurred images, backlight bleeding and other picture-quality concerns.

07 / CONTROLS

Remote control issues

Troubleshooting when your TV does not respond to its remote or control inputs as expected.

08 / CONNECTIVITY

HDMI connectivity issues

Help identifying signal, input and HDMI connection problems between your TV and devices.

09 / POWER

TV power on/off problems

Assessment when your television will not start, switches off unexpectedly or has an inconsistent power issue.

10 / COMPONENTS

Motherboard repair

Diagnosis of television motherboard faults and advice on the available repair options.

11 / COMPONENTS

Chip-level repair

Component-level troubleshooting for supported television boards, based on the issue and model.

12 / DIAGNOSIS

General TV troubleshooting

Not sure what is wrong? Describe the symptoms and we can help you figure out a sensible first step.
