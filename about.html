A LITTLE ABOUT US

Repair work is
personal work.

V.K Electronics is an independent LED and LCD TV repair center at 266/K, Ward No. 2 in Mehrauli. For more than 16 years, we have worked hands-on with televisions and the problems that can interrupt the way you watch them.

We believe good service begins by listening. A clear description of what is happening helps us understand the issue; a careful assessment helps us explain what can be done. We aim to make that process straightforward, respectful and grounded in the facts.

Whether your TV has a display fault, a power issue or a connection that will not cooperate, we welcome the chance to help you find a sensible next step.
